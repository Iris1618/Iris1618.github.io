Rui Lin Lab · NIBS, Beijing | Summer Research Intern | June–August 2026

Two summer projects combining microglia transplantation, tissue analysis, and in vivo imaging.

PROJECT 01

Establish whole-brain microglial gene editing

Primary Microglia Transplantation (pMGT) into Adult Mice

This project explored primary microglia transplantation into adult mice as an approach toward whole-brain microglial gene editing. The workflow combined primary cell isolation and culture, AAV-mediated editing, and transplantation, followed by tissue staining and flow cytometry to evaluate engraftment.

Workflow

My contributions
  • Performed primary microglia isolation and culture and AAV-mediated gene editing.
  • Carried out oral gavage and stereotaxic injections as part of the transplantation experiments.
  • Conducted mouse perfusion, brain sectioning, immunofluorescence staining, and flow cytometry to evaluate microglial engraftment.
What I learned

I gained experience across the workflow from cell preparation to tissue-level evaluation, and learned to use complementary methods when assessing transplanted cells.

PROJECT 02

Investigate CD39 regulation of injury-induced ATP signaling

CD39 overexpression (CD39-OE) with two-photon imaging

This project investigated the role of CD39 in injury-induced ATP signaling using CD39 overexpression and in vivo two-photon imaging. AAV delivery and cranial window preparation enabled fluorescence imaging of the experimental readouts in the living brain.

Workflow

My contributions
  • Performed stereotaxic AAV injections and cranial window surgery.
  • Gained hands-on experience in two-photon microscopy and examined individual and merged fluorescence channels.
  • Presented imaging observations and possible technical explanations in my internship report.
What I learned

I developed hands-on skills in cranial window preparation and two-photon microscopy, and learned the importance of assessing imaging quality before interpreting fluorescence signals.
